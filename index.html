ABOUT STRAY BULLET

Stray Bullet is a tabletop roleplaying game set against a fictionalized Cold War.

Players take the role of agents operating in a world of espionage, political tension, covert operations and dangerous secrets.

Information is a weapon. Trust is a liability. Every operation can change the course of the mission.

Stray Bullet features a tactical combat system, characters with a wide range of skills with different levels of competence where it matters who performs the task and how they prepare, and the Prototype system, which allows specialization and obtaining skills capable of changing the course of the operation.Stray Bullet features a tactical combat system, characters with a wide range of skills with different levels of competence where it matters who performs the task and how they prepare, and the Prototype system, which allows specialization and obtaining skills capable of changing the course of the operation.
