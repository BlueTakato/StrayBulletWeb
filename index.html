ABOUT STRAY BULLET

Stray Bullet is a tabletop roleplaying game set in a fictionalized Cold War.

Players take the role of agents operating in a world of espionage, political tension, covert operations and dangerous secrets.

Information is a weapon. Trust is a liability. Every operation can change the course of the mission.

Stray Bullet features a tactical combat system where strategy matters.

Characters have a wide range of skills with different levels of competence, where who performs a task and how they prepare matters.

The Prototype system allows characters to specialize and obtain unique abilities capable of changing the course of an operation.

No AI will be used to generate content for Stray Bullet. All content is created by the development team.
